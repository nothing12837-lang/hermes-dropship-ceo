# E-commerce Marketing & Copywriting Skill

## Purpose
Enables Hermes to act as Chief Marketing Officer for the dropshipping store, creating viral hooks, persuasive product pages, and automated customer retention campaigns.

## Key Modules
1. **High-Converting Product Descriptions:**
   - Problem $\rightarrow$ Agitate $\rightarrow$ Solution framework.
   - Bulleted benefit lists, trust guarantees, and clear Call-to-Actions (CTA).
2. **Ad Creatives & Hooks:**
   - TikTok/Reels UGC-style scriptwriting with first 3-second viral hooks.
   - Facebook/Instagram carousel ad copy focused on impulse-buy psychology.
3. **Email Sequences (Klaviyo / Resend):**
   - 3-step Abandoned Cart Recovery (sent at 1 hour, 12 hours, 24 hours).
   - Post-purchase thank you & cross-sell flows.
